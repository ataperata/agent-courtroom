Review of the pilot design, written July 2 at leadership's request.

The whole team switched schedules at once. There was no control group and no staggered start. The pilot quarter also included the mobile app launch, which changed both ticket volume and ticket mix.

Without a control group, the effect of the schedule cannot be separated from the effect of the launch.

Recommendation: run a second pilot with half the team on each schedule for one quarter.
