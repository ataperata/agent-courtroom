---
id: four-day-week
title: The Four-Day Week Pilot
claim: The four-day work week pilot increased the support team's throughput.
burden: preponderance
prosecution_theory: Tickets closed per week went up during the pilot, the team reports better focus, and customer satisfaction held steady. Fewer days did not mean less work done.
defense_theory: Ticket counts went up because a product launch drove more tickets, not because the team got faster. Response times got worse, reopen rates climbed, and the pilot had no control group.
---
From April through June the 14-person customer support team worked Monday to Thursday, ten hours a day. Leadership wants to know whether to make the schedule permanent. The pilot charter defined success as higher throughput with no loss in service quality.

The same quarter included the launch of the mobile app, which roughly doubled the number of new customers.
