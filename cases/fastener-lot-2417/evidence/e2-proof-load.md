Axial proof load test, September 4. Eight bolts from box 9, chosen because box 9 produced the low hardness readings.

- Required proof load for M8 x 1.25, class 10.9: 30.2 kN
- Results: 31.0, 30.9, 29.1, 31.4, 28.7, 30.8, 29.6, 31.2 kN

Three of the eight bolts tested yielded before reaching the 30.2 kN proof load.

Fracture surfaces were not examined. Bolts from other boxes were not proof tested.
