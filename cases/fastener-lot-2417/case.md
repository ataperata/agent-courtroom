Lot 2417 is 12 boxes of M8 x 1.25 flange bolts, property class 10.9, for the wheel hub assembly line. Receiving inspection flagged the lot on September 3. Production needs a disposition within the week: accept, sort, or reject and return.

A reject sends the full lot back and pauses the hub line for about four days while a replacement ships. Accepting a nonconforming lot puts under-strength fasteners into a safety-relevant joint. The material review board decides on the preponderance of the evidence, and the supplier has already disputed the receiving results.
